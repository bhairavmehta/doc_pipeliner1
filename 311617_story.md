Context for the writer:
- Purpose: the pipeline reads aviation safety reports and organizes them into a tree of risk categories. Level 1 of the tree is the ICAO/CICTT occurrence categories; the pipeline discovers the sub-categories below them from the report text. The result feeds the SEWG (Safety Enhancement Working Group) top-down decomposition charts, such as the pilot chart RI → landing/takeoff on unintended surfaces.
- Audience: the Safety AI/ML data science team, safety subject-matter experts and the SEWG lead. Keep the language simple.
- Builds on: US 311610 (ICAO occurrence category reference), US 311613 (industry safety taxonomy survey), the data-source review, and the research findings provided.
- Safety SMEs must review and approve every category before it is published. AI may propose; people decide.
